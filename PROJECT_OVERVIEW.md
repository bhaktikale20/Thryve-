# 🌟 Personalized Wellness & Habit Coach with Mood Tracking

A comprehensive wellness application designed to help you build healthy habits, track your mood, practice meditation, and achieve your wellness goals through gamification.

## ✨ Features

### 1. **User Onboarding Flow**
- **Login Page**: Welcoming entry point with gradient design
- **Mood Tracking**: Select your daily mood with interactive emoji-based options
- **Habit Selection**: Choose from pre-built habits or create custom ones
- **Personalized Wellness Setup**: Configure your wellness preferences and goals

### 2. **Meditation & Mindfulness**
- **Guided Meditation Sessions**: Multiple meditation types including:
  - Breathing exercises (5 min)
  - Mindfulness practice (10 min)
  - Gratitude reflection (7 min)
  - Deep relaxation (15 min)
- **Interactive Timer**: Visual breathing guidance with animations
- **Progress Tracking**: Earn XP for completed sessions

### 3. **Gamification System**
- **Level System**: Progress through levels by earning XP
- **Points & Rewards**: Earn points for:
  - Completing daily check-ins
  - Maintaining streaks
  - Finishing meditation sessions
  - Completing challenges
- **Achievements**: Unlock badges including:
  - First Step (10 XP)
  - 3 Day Streak (25 XP)
  - Habit Master (30 XP)
  - Meditation Guru (50 XP)
  - Week Warrior (50 XP)
  - Habit Hero (100 XP)
  - Wellness Champion (150 XP)
  - Mindful Master (200 XP)

### 4. **Wellness Challenges & Games**
- **Daily Challenges**: Complete wellness tasks for bonus points
  - Hydration Hero (10 XP)
  - Step Master (25 XP)
  - Zen Warrior (20 XP)
  - Sleep Champion (30 XP)
- **Wellness Trivia**: Test your knowledge with health and wellness questions
- **Interactive Learning**: Educational content about wellness best practices

### 5. **Habit Calendar**
- **Visual Calendar**: See your journey at a glance
- **Streak Tracking**: Monitor your consistency with streak counters
- **Historical Data**: Review past entries and mood patterns
- **Completion Metrics**: Track your habit completion rate

### 6. **Personalized Dashboard**
- **Quick Stats**: View mood, habits, and wellness score
- **Personalized Recommendations**: Get tailored suggestions based on:
  - Current mood
  - Stress levels
  - Sleep patterns
  - Activity levels
- **Quick Actions**: Easy access to all features
- **Progress Overview**: See your level, XP, and streak

### 7. **Insights & Analytics**
- **Weekly Mood Trends**: Visualize mood patterns over time
- **Habit Completion Rates**: Track which habits you're mastering
- **Key Metrics**: Monitor streaks, check-ins, and top habits
- **Personalized Insights**: Receive AI-powered wellness recommendations

## 🎨 Design Features

- **Beautiful Gradients**: Stunning color transitions throughout the app
- **Responsive Layout**: Works perfectly on mobile and desktop
- **Smooth Animations**: Engaging transitions and interactions
- **Icon System**: Clear visual language using Lucide React icons
- **Color-Coded Elements**: Intuitive color associations for different features

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Create Your Account**: Enter your name and email
2. **Track Your Mood**: Select how you're feeling today
3. **Choose Habits**: Pick habits you want to build
4. **Personalize**: Configure your wellness preferences
5. **Start Your Journey**: Access your dashboard and begin!

## 📊 Gamification Elements

### XP & Levels
- Complete setup: 50 XP
- Daily check-in: 20 XP
- Meditation session: 2 XP per minute
- Challenge completion: 10-30 XP
- Trivia correct answer: 15-20 XP
- Level up every 100 XP

### Streaks
- Track consecutive days of activity
- Visual flame indicator
- Motivational milestones

### Achievements
- 8 unique achievements to unlock
- Progress bars for locked achievements
- Bonus XP rewards

## 💡 Tips for Success

1. Start with 2-3 habits and build from there
2. Maintain your daily streak for maximum motivation
3. Complete meditation sessions to reduce stress
4. Review your calendar regularly to spot patterns
5. Challenge yourself with daily wellness games
6. Unlock all achievements for maximum XP

## 🏆 Achievement Guide

- **First Step**: Complete your first wellness check-in
- **3 Day Streak**: Build a 3-day consistency habit
- **Habit Master**: Complete all selected habits in one day
- **Meditation Guru**: Finish 10 meditation sessions
- **Week Warrior**: Maintain a 7-day streak
- **Habit Hero**: Track habits for 30 consecutive days
- **Wellness Champion**: Reach level 10
- **Mindful Master**: Complete 50 meditation sessions

## 🔒 Privacy

All data is stored locally in your browser. Your wellness journey is completely private and secure.
